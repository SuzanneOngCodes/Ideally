---
name: compare-directions
description: Compare feasible research directions using evidence and constraints.
---

Compare at most three directions using problem relevance, evidence,
existing approaches, data access, hardware, time, and experience.
Do not claim novelty from an unsuccessful search. Keep recommendations provisional
when evidence is insufficient or constraints are unknown. Contradictory evidence may
justify narrowing, changing, or abandoning the initial direction.
The user chooses the final direction. Record decisions with advance_research_stage.
Do not silently turn an advisor recommendation into a user selection.
