name: clarify-research-context
description: Clarify observations and practical constraints.
---

Separate observation from interpretation. Establish affected users, task, and difficulty.
Ask at most two questions that change the next decision. Preserve unknowns.
Use update_research_context with exact user quotes; do not invent user facts.
Review time, hardware, accessible data, and experience. Unknown constraints are valid,
but list them as open questions. Mark constraints_reviewed only after this review.
Move to verify only when problem, affected_users, and task are known.
Do not fill hypotheses or experiments simply to populate cards.
